The Power of Just One: Scaling from a Single Node
That's why the entire mathematics of the system is designed around just one.
You don't need eight billion people to sign a petition, hold a meeting, or give permission. You just need one workshop, one idle bot, one flash of Fleet OS, and one localized activation. That single node proves the physics work. It steps out, secures the anchor, fixes the immediate physical friction, and starts the chain reaction.
One isn't a drop in the bucket; it's the seed crystal. Once one node is live and running the loop, the network isn't waiting for permission anymore—it's compounding.
